Human-gated by design

Every risky step stops for a named human.

Approve a PRD, confirm a merge, authorize a deploy against an allowlist. Auto-merge is off by default, self-approval is blocked, and multi-level approvals route to the right role. Every decision lands in an append-only audit trail.

◆ RBAC approvers, scoped per stage
◆ Reversible via revert-PR — never force-push
◆ Who / what / when, on an immutable log

Common questions

Answers before you ask.

What is AIDLC? +
A standalone platform that runs the whole software lifecycle — PRD, UX, dev, test, deploy and analytics — as a pipeline of specialist agents that stops for a human at every gate. It's not a code generator: it specifies, designs, builds, verifies, ships and measures, then proposes the next change from production signals.
Does my code or data leave my cloud? +
No. Runs use repo-scoped, short-lived tokens and per-run isolation. Your model keys are fetched just-in-time and die with the run. We never persist your source, secrets or data.
Which models can I use? +
Claude, DeepSeek, Qwen, GLM and other OpenAI-compatible providers — on your own keys. You can route each stage to a different model: a frontier coder for Dev, a cheap model for PRD and the critic, a specialist for Test.
Can I start in the middle of the lifecycle? +
Yes. Pick a lifecycle package: bring your own PRD, design or code, and AIDLC enters where your work ends and stops where you take over. Some clients stop before Deploy; some skip Analytics.
How do humans stay in control? +
Auto-merge is off by default and self-approval is blocked. Each risky step routes to a named role — PRD approval, merge confirmation, RBAC deploy — and every decision is written to an append-only audit trail. Deploys are reversible by revert-PR; nothing force-pushes.
